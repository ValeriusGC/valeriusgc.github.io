Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026

Wordamajig is a children's word-mixing game. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app and how you can contact the developer about privacy.

1. Information stored on your device

Wordamajig lets you create custom word collections and add photos. Your custom collections, photos, favorites, and game preferences are stored locally on your device. The developer does not operate an account system or a server that receives this content.

Depending on your device settings, the operating system or your backup provider may include app data in device backups. Those backups are controlled by the relevant provider, not by Wordamajig.

2. Photos and device access

If you choose to add a photo, the app uses the photo you select to create your collection. Photos are not uploaded to a developer-operated server. You can use the game without adding personal photos.

3. Purchases

Wordamajig may offer a one-time in-app purchase to unlock unlimited custom collections. Where available, purchases are processed by the app store (for example, Huawei AppGallery). The store may process transaction and account information under its own privacy policy. The developer does not receive your full payment-card details. The app may receive information needed to determine whether the feature has been purchased or restored.

4. Analytics, advertising, and tracking

The current version does not include third-party analytics, advertising networks, or a developer-operated crash-reporting service. Wordamajig does not use your custom collections or photos for advertising or profiling. App stores and operating systems may separately process technical information as described in their own policies.

5. Children and parents

Wordamajig is designed for children. It does not provide public profiles, chat, or sharing of custom collections with other players. Purchases, if offered, are intended to be accessed through a parental gate. Parents should supervise any purchases and decide which photos children may add.

6. Retention and deletion

Custom collections and photos remain on your device until you delete them in the app, clear its data, or uninstall it, subject to device backups. Because this content is not held on the developer's server, the developer cannot remotely access or delete it. Uninstalling the app may not delete copies stored in device backups.

7. Your privacy rights

You or your parent/guardian may ask questions about privacy or request access, correction, or deletion of any personal information that the developer may hold. Contact frostyland@gmail.com. We will review and respond to requests in accordance with applicable law. For locally stored data, deletion must be performed on your device. For store-account or payment records, please contact the relevant app store directly.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app's features, integrations, or data practices change. The current version will be published at this URL with an updated effective date.
